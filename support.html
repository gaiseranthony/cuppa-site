How the caffeine number works

Cuppa adds up the caffeine of everything you have logged and lets each dose decay with a five-hour half-life — the textbook average for healthy adults. It is an estimate. People clear caffeine at very different rates (genetics, pregnancy, smoking, some medicines, and the contraceptive pill all change it), and two espressos from the same machine can differ by a third. Use the number as a guide, not a measurement, and talk to a doctor about your own limit.

Sleep-ready and last call

Sleep-ready is when the estimate falls under 50 mg, read against the bedtime you set. Last call is the latest time one more of your usual would still clear by that bedtime. Change your bedtime and limit on the You screen.

The widget's + button

Tapping + on the widget logs your usual straight away and saves it the next time you open Cuppa. It needs iOS 17 or later. If the widget's label reads an old name, remove and re-add the widget.

Places and cafés

With location on, Cuppa names the café you are in from Apple Maps. If it guesses wrong, tap the place on the Places tab and rename it — your own name wins from then on, for every visit. Location is optional; you can type any name when you log.

Apple Health

You → Settings → Connect Health writes each coffee's caffeine to Health and reads your sleep back so History can compare nights after lighter and heavier days. Cuppa needs four nights on each side before it says anything about the comparison. Revoke access any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Backups and a new phone

You → Backup → Back up now saves one file with everything, photos included, anywhere you like. On the new phone, choose Have a backup? Restore it on the welcome screen, or Restore on the You screen later. A restore never removes anything already on the phone.

Deleting everything

You → Delete all my data. It is immediate and permanent, so back up first if there is any chance you will want it back.
