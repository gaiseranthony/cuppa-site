Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026

The short version: Cuppa has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything you log stays on your phone. We never see it, and we could not sell it if we wanted to.

Who we are

Cuppa is an iPhone app for logging coffee and estimating caffeine, made by Anthony Gaiser ("we"). Questions about this policy go to gaiseranthony@gmail.com.

What Cuppa stores, and where

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else by the app.

  • What you log: drinks, times, sizes, ratings, notes, recipes, bags of beans, and photos you attach.
  • Places: the name of where you drank a coffee and, if location is on, its coordinates and city.
  • Settings: your usual order, bedtime, daily limit, reminder time, appearance, and whether Apple Health is connected.
  • Your subscription status: a note of which plan is active, kept in step with the App Store.

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted iTunes/Finder backup, iOS may include Cuppa's data in that backup like any other app's. That is between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions, and what each one is used for

Location (optional)

Only if you turn it on. When you log a coffee, Cuppa takes one location fix to record where you were, and asks Apple Maps for the street and for cafés near that point so the place can be named. The coordinates are stored with that coffee on your device. The lookup goes to Apple in the same way the Maps app's does and is covered by Apple's privacy policy; nothing is sent to us. Cuppa never tracks your location in the background.

Apple Health (optional)

Only if you connect it from the You screen. With your permission Cuppa writes each coffee's caffeine to Health as a dietary caffeine sample, and reads your sleep analysis so it can show how your nights compare after lighter and heavier coffee days. Health data is read into the app's memory to draw those screens and is not stored by Cuppa, not transmitted anywhere, and never used for advertising, marketing, or sold or shared with any third party. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps, and remove the caffeine samples Cuppa wrote from there.

Camera and photo library (optional)

Only when you choose to attach a photo to a coffee. The photo is copied into Cuppa's storage on your device. Cuppa does not read the rest of your library.

Notifications (optional)

The streak reminder is a local notification scheduled on your phone. Cuppa uses no push notification service and has no server that could send one.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store and handled by Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and holds your payment details; Cuppa never sees them. The app receives only Apple's confirmation that a subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Apple's terms and privacy policy apply to the purchase.

Things you send out of the app yourself

  • Share cards: when you tap Share, Cuppa makes an image and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice.
  • Backups: Back up now creates one file containing everything you have logged, including photos, and hands it to the share sheet for you to save where you like. Anyone with that file can restore it, so treat it like the data it contains.

The home-screen widget

The widget shows your caffeine estimate and can log your usual. It shares data with the app through a private container on your device that only Cuppa and its widget can read.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-ups, or logins.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No server of ours receives anything from the app.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data — we do not have it.

Apple may collect standard diagnostics and App Store analytics under its own policy and your iOS settings; that is Apple's, not ours.

Deleting your data

You → Delete all my data erases everything Cuppa stores, immediately and permanently. Deleting the app from your phone does the same. Caffeine samples written to Apple Health stay in Health until you remove them there. Cancelling a subscription is done through your Apple ID settings.

Children

Cuppa is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app has no server, a change in policy can never retroactively affect data we never received.
